# Using WBSK Studio

Nov 1, 2026 · @Siddhant

Everything on wbsk.in is added and changed here, without code. Nothing you do reaches the website until you press Publish.

*Draft written from the approved planning decisions. Screenshots and exact button names are added once WBSK Studio is built.*

## Finding your way

- **The menu follows the website's pages:** Homepage, Weddings, Films, Pre-wedding (Destinations, Shoots), Our team, Careers. Then Couples' words (Reviews, Reaction videos), Forms (Enquiry forms, Packages) and Site settings.
- **Home** is where you start each day:
  - **Quick actions** — Add a wedding, Add a film, Add a review, and more.
  - **Waiting for you** — drafts, and reviews not yet approved.
  - **Worth a look** — small things to fix, like an old Google rating.
  - **Last published** — your latest change and whether it is live.
- **Top bar:** shows if the website is live or updating, and has **Guide** (this page and the export guides).
- **Tutorials:** a short film of the whole Studio. Start here if you're new.
- **Search:** press **Cmd+K** anywhere and type a task, a page or a couple's name to go straight there.

## Add a wedding

Open **Weddings → + New wedding** and fill the five tabs, in any order. Your work saves itself as a draft; a tick shows when a tab is done.

1. **Couple** — names, Destination or Local, city, venue, wedding date. The page address (e.g. `/client/meera-arjun`) is made for you.
2. **Photos**
   - **Card photo** 4:5, at least 1600 × 2000.
   - **Desktop cover** 21:9, at least 2560 px wide.
   - **Phone cover** 3:4, at least 1500 × 2000.
   - Too small? The Studio refuses it and tells you the size needed.
   - **Focal point:** click on the couple. Keep faces **above the pale band** — the name label sits there.
   - **Gallery:** drop 50–60 photos at once, then drag to reorder. A **Small** badge means under 2560 px; it can stay but may look soft full-screen.
   - **Every photo needs a description**, e.g. "Meera and Arjun laughing during the varmala, Goa". Publishing waits until all are done. Allow 10–15 minutes.
3. **Story** — a short description (about 120 characters, shown under the card) and "Their story" (60–120 words).
4. **Films & reels** — teaser and highlight film as YouTube links, **each with its 16:9 poster**; Instagram reel links with an optional label (e.g. "Sangeet").
5. **Homepage** — switch on **Featured** to show the card on the homepage.

Photos come from Lightroom first: follow the **Photo Export Guide**.

## Check it, then publish

1. **Phone preview** sits beside the form and changes as you type. Only you can see drafts.
2. Press **Open full size ↗** to check the desktop version — the wide cover and the gallery. Press Esc to come back.
3. The bar at the bottom lists anything **left before publishing**. When it is empty, press **Publish**.
4. You will see **"Publishing… the website updates in about 3–5 minutes."** Keep working if you like.
5. When it says **"Live on the website ✓"**, press **Open page ↗** and look at it on your phone.

**If it says "The website didn't update":** your work is safe and the website still shows the last good version. Press **Try again**. If it fails again, tell Siddhant.

## Taking things down

Nothing you take down is lost; you can switch it back on any time.

| Button | Use it for | What happens on the website |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Archive** | Older weddings and destinations | Off the lists and homepage; its own page still works for Google and old links |
| **Remove from website** | When a couple asks | Gone everywhere; its address leads to the Weddings (or Pre-wedding) page |
| **Hide from website** | Films, reviews, reaction videos, shoots, team members, roles | Not shown anywhere |

**Never delete.** Permanent deletion is for the owner only.

## Other everyday jobs

| Job | Where | Remember |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a film | Films → + New | YouTube link + 16:9 poster; **Featured** = homepage, **Show on Films page** = Films page; drag to order |
| Add a review | Reviews → + New | Ask the couple's permission first; switch on **Approved** or it won't show |
| Add a reaction video | Reaction videos → + New | YouTube link + 9:16 poster |
| Add a pre-wedding shoot | Pre-wedding → Shoots → + New | Pick its destination(s) and Full-length or Concept; the destination page fills itself |
| Add a destination | Pre-wedding → Destinations → + New | B&W transparent PNG + two covers; its record appears once it has a published shoot |
| Add a team member | Our team → + New | B&W 4:5 portrait, already styled in Lightroom |
| Update open roles | Careers | Roles also appear in the careers form |
| Update the Google rating | Site settings | Rating, review count and link; check monthly |
| Change small wording | Homepage (Hero video, Who we are, Get in touch); Weddings → Weddings page text (every couple's closing lines); Careers → Careers page text | Leave a field empty to keep the usual wording |

## Cover videos

Homepage → Hero video, or Pre-wedding → Cover video. Replace **all four together**: desktop video, phone video, desktop poster, phone poster. Files over 30 MB are refused. Follow the **Cover Video Export Guide**.

## Google & sharing (optional)

Titles, descriptions and the share image are written for you. Open **Google & sharing** only to reword an important page; leave it empty otherwise.

## When to ask for help

You can change any words, photos, videos, order and switches. A new kind of page, a new section or a design change goes to the owner.
